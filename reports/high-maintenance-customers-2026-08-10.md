# High-Maintenance Customer Report — one-off
**Window:** jobs submitted 10 Feb – 9 Aug 2026 (6 months) · customers with 2+ jobs (38 customers)
**Data source:** live production system — job chat messages, logo approval timings, invoiced values.

## How the score works
Each customer is measured per job and ranked against every other customer (0–100 percentile, higher = more effort):
- **Questions asked (30%)** — staff messages sent before the job is approved (proxy for incomplete info)
- **Message volume (25%)** — customer messages per job (company average: 5.0/job)
- **Chasing (25%)** — customer messages after the job is approved (company average: 4.2/job)
- **Completeness (20%)** — average days from submission to logo approval

## What stands out
- **Keeps London (94)** — 18.7 messages per job on £70 orders, 14 of them chasing after approval. The single most effort-intensive customer per pound.
- **Bolt Uniforms (83)** — worst on incomplete submissions: staff send 7.3 questions per job (company average 1.7) on orders worth just £19 each.
- **BRT Sports Supplies (82)** — the highest-volume problem account: 13 jobs, 3 questions per job and 6+ chase messages per job. Fixing this one relationship saves the most staff time.
- **Kreative Embroidery & Design (75)** — takes nearly 15 days to approve logos, then sends 14 chase messages per job. Their delay creates their own chasing.
- **Your best big customers prove it's possible:** PC Sports (54 jobs, score 15), Rutland Merchandise (38 jobs, 29), East Yorkshire Workwear (22 jobs, 23) barely message at all — under 1 message per job.

## Education angle — who gets which message
- **"Submit a complete order" guide:** Bolt Uniforms, BRT Sports, Harrogate Embroidery, Merch4, The Alternative Store — their jobs arrive missing info.
- **Proactive status updates instead of chasing:** Keeps London, Customised Prints & Embroidery, Kreative, PRINT MATTERS KENT, JS Branded Clothing — they mostly chase after booking.
- **Logo approval nudges:** BPM Print & Workwear (24 days), Creations 4 You (19 days), Kreative (15 days), Branding Inc / Uniforms For Work (9 days) — their own approval delay holds jobs up.

## Caveats
- "Questions" and "chasing" are inferred from message timing (before/after job approval), not message content — a chatty-but-easy customer can score higher than they deserve.
- Customers with only 2–3 jobs (Keeps London, Harrogate, Merch4) have small samples; one awkward order skews their score.
- Internal staff notes and deleted messages are excluded.
