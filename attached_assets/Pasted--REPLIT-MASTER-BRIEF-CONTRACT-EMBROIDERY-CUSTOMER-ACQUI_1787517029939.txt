# REPLIT MASTER BRIEF — CONTRACT EMBROIDERY CUSTOMER ACQUISITION LANDING PAGE

## Objective

Create/rebuild the landing page at:

**https://production.selectbranding.co.uk/demo-access**

This will become the primary customer acquisition landing page for the **Contract Embroidery Division of Select Branding Solutions**.

Initially we will drive traffic to this page through targeted cold email outreach.

We will then use the same landing page for Google Ads and potentially other paid advertising.

This is NOT primarily a demo page for our production portal.

The portal is an important part of our proposition, but the main thing we are selling is:

# Production capacity.

More specifically:

**We enable businesses selling branded clothing, workwear, sportswear, schoolwear and merchandise to grow their sales without having to build the embroidery production infrastructure themselves.**

The visitor should leave the page understanding:

**You sell it. We embroider it.**
