---
name: Awaiting garments reminder policy
description: Why customers get weekly reminders for long-standing jobs with no line items.
---

Email customers weekly about jobs that have remained in “Awaiting Line Items — Not Yet Booked In” for at least seven days.

**Why:** The user says these jobs generally indicate that garments have not been received. It is not proof that garments are missing.

**How to apply:** Ask for garment delivery updates or tracking details without asserting non-receipt as fact. Stop garment reminders when receipt is recorded or jobs move out of that queue. Keep the existing app structure and timer scheduling.
