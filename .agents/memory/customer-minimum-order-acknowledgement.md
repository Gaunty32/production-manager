---
name: Customer minimum-order acknowledgement
description: Business rule for the customer-facing warning on submissions below the six-unit minimum.
---

From 1 September 2026, every customer submission with a stated quantity of 1–5 units must require acceptance of the minimum-order notice.

Do not try to exempt initials/name jobs in the customer submission form. That form does not classify job types, and staff identifies and handles the initials exemption later.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a consistent warning for every under-six customer submission rather than adding a customer-facing job-type field or guessing from the job name.

**How to apply:** Keep the customer warning based only on the effective date and submitted quantity. Apply the actual initials/name billing exemption later, where staff assigns the line-item job type.
